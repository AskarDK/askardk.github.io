Product thinking.
Engineering detail.

I enjoy the intersection of product and engineering: turning an audio stream into a useful hint, connecting a web application to Telegram, or helping people come together around local events.

01

The whole journey

From signing in and taking a first action to a saved result. The backend and interface need to work together.

02

States matter

Errors, repeated requests, data access and process shutdown are part of the product.

03

Clarity over complexity
